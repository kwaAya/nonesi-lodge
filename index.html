Good to Know

Questions guests usually ask.

Standard check-in is from 14:00 to 20:00, and check-out is by 10:00. Arriving later in the evening? Just let us know in advance on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a smooth arrival.

A hearty breakfast is available on request — just let us know when you book or message us on WhatsApp. The lodge also has full self-catering facilities in every room (fridge, microwave, kettle) if you prefer to sort yourself out.

Absolutely — children of all ages are welcome. We're a quiet, family-friendly lodge. Cribs and extra beds aren't available, and pets aren't permitted on the premises.

Card and cashless payments — Visa, Mastercard, and Maestro. Cash is not accepted on-site.

Cancellation terms depend on the room and dates — we'll communicate these clearly upfront. We're a quiet family lodge; we don't accommodate bachelor, bachelorette, or similar party groups.
