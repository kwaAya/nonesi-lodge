The Lodge

Comfort you can
actually settle into.

Nonesi Lodge is a B&B and self-catering lodge in Southbourne, Komani. Friendly staff, a relaxed feel, and Queenstown's shops and restaurants close by.

Whether you're here for work, a family occasion, or just passing through the Eastern Cape — we welcome you like a neighbour, not a reservation number.

  • Check-in from 14:00  ·  Check-out by 11:00
  • Free private parking & Wi-Fi throughout
  • Outdoor swimming pool & well-groomed garden
  • Daily housekeeping, laundry & ironing
  • Event & function venue setup available
  • Security alarm, heating, non-smoking rooms
  • Visa, Mastercard, Maestro & cashless payments
  • Quiet, family-friendly — no parties or groups
