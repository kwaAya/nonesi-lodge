Friendly accommodation, practical comfort, and a convenient Komani location.

Nonesi Lodge is a B&B and self-catering lodge in Southbourne. Friendly staff and a relaxed, homely feel mean you're welcomed rather than just checked in, whether you're here for work, a family occasion, or a stop on an Eastern Cape journey.

Garden surroundings, a quieter lodge-style setting, and Queenstown's shops, restaurants and business areas close by.

  • Check-in from 14:00  |  Check-out by 11:00
  • Free private parking & free Wi-Fi in all areas
  • Daily housekeeping, laundry & ironing services
  • Well-groomed garden, terrace & heating
  • Security alarm & non-smoking rooms (smoking area available)
  • Payments: Visa, Mastercard, Maestro & cashless
  • Family-friendly — no bachelor/bachelorette parties
