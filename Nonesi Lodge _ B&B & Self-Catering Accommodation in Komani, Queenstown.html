SOUTHBOURNE · KOMANI

Nonesi Lodge is a B&B and self-catering lodge in Southbourne. Friendly staff and a relaxed, homely feel mean you're welcomed rather than just checked in. We offer an outdoor swimming pool, a well-groomed garden and terrace, and an intimate function venue setup — whether you're here for work, a family occasion, or a stop on an Eastern Cape journey.

Garden surroundings, a quieter lodge-style setting, and Queenstown's shops, restaurants and business areas close by.
